Privacy Policy

Duck Days for iPhone

← Support

Last updated 6 September 2026

Duck Days does not collect any data.

What the app stores

The countdown you create — its title, its date, the duck you picked, and whether the duck moves — is saved on your device in an App Group container shared between Duck Days and its home screen widget. That is the only thing the app writes, and it never leaves your iPhone.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising.
  • No tracking, and no data shared with third parties.
  • No network requests of any kind. The app contains no networking code.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, calendar, microphone, or camera.

Deleting your data

Deleting Duck Days removes the App Group container and everything in it. There is nothing stored anywhere else, so there is nothing else to delete and no request to make.

Children

Duck Days is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes, the new policy will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Since the app has no network access, a change here would mean a change to the app itself, shipped as an update you choose to install.
