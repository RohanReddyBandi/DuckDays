# Privacy Policy — Duck Days

_Last updated: 2 September 2026_

Duck Days does not collect any data.

## What the app stores

The countdown you create — its title, its date, the duck you picked, and whether the
duck moves — is saved on your device in an App Group container shared between Duck Days
and its home screen widget. That is the only thing the app writes, and it never leaves
your iPhone.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts, no sign-in.
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising.
- No tracking, and no data shared with third parties.
- No network requests of any kind. The app contains no networking code.
- No access to your contacts, photos, location, calendar, microphone, or camera.

## Deleting your data

Deleting Duck Days removes the App Group container and everything in it. There is
nothing stored anywhere else, so there is nothing else to delete and no request to make.

## Children

Duck Days is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, children included.
